Let’s get you playing

How can we help?

A quick setup guide and answers to the most common questions.

Start your first round

  1. Open the game library and choose Fruit Slash Party, the free game.
  2. Place your iPhone or iPad securely in landscape, facing the play area. Allow camera access when prompted.
  3. Enter Body Check. Show your head, shoulders and hips first, then follow the current hand or movement step. You do not need every joint visible at once.
  4. Start a round, sweep your hands across the fruit and avoid bombs. For touch play, choose the visible touch fallback and swipe the screen.

Keep the play area clear and leave room between players. An adult should supervise children.

Setup and troubleshooting

The body check will not finish

Read the current step and progress indicator. Keep your head, shoulders and hips visible first, then show only the inputs requested for that game. Improve lighting, keep the device stable and step into the camera’s view. If you leave the frame, a step may need to be repeated. You can choose touch controls if the setup is unsuitable.

Camera access is off

Open More to check camera access. Use Open iOS Settings when offered, then allow camera access for MotionPlay. Return to the app and start the body check again. Device restrictions may prevent access. Touch controls remain available without camera permission.

I want to use touch controls

Choose the touch fallback on the preparation/body-check screen. Each game shows its touch controls. During a session, switching back to body controls opens calibration and pauses play while you prepare. Touch selection does not necessarily stop an already running camera; revoke camera permission in system Settings if you do not want camera access.

How do I play on a TV?

Put your device and compatible AirPlay receiver on a suitable network. Open Control Center, choose Screen Mirroring and select your receiver. MotionPlay shows setup help under More → TV setup. The app cannot open or choose the system mirror connection for you. For connection problems, follow Apple’s AirPlay guide. Receiver and network conditions affect delay.

If a game uses the camera as its background, that background appears on the mirrored display too. Stop Screen Mirroring from Control Center when finished.

Which games are free?

Fruit Slash Party is free. The other six games require MotionPlay Pass (monthly or yearly) or the one-time lifetime unlock. Current local prices are shown in the App Store purchase sheet.

I paid, but a game is locked

Check that the device uses the Apple Account that purchased access, then open More → Restore purchases. An active subscription or lifetime purchase should restore verified access. If a transaction is pending, wait for Apple to finish it; do not buy again to troubleshoot. For billing problems, visit Apple billing support.

How do I cancel a subscription or request a refund?

Open Apple Account subscription settings to manage or cancel a subscription. Deleting MotionPlay does not cancel it. For refund requests, use Apple’s Report a Problem. The lifetime unlock is a one-time purchase and does not renew.

Does MotionPlay record or upload my camera?

The developer does not receive camera frames. MotionPlay processes camera frames and movement data on device, without saving them or sending them to a developer server. The bundled games receive temporary coordinates and gesture flags. User-selected screen mirroring or AirPlay can send the visible camera background to your receiver, and system screen recording can record it. Read the Privacy Policy.
