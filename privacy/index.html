Privacy Policy

Revised October 30, 2026 · Applies to MotionPlay for iPhone and iPad

MotionPlay uses your device’s camera to turn body movement into game controls. The app does not require a MotionPlay account, include advertising or third-party analytics, or send camera or body-tracking data to a developer server.

Camera & movementPurchasesYour controlsContact

Camera and movement data

Camera frames are processed in memory on your device to estimate body joints and game controls. Raw camera frames are never provided to a game. Temporary normalized joint coordinates and gesture flags are provided in memory to the currently selected, bundled game. MotionPlay does not save camera frames, body coordinates or motion history, send them to the developer, or use them for advertising or identity recognition.

Closing a game session releases the capture pipeline. Choosing touch controls during an existing session does not necessarily stop the camera, because you may switch back to body controls.

When you mirror your screen: a native camera background may appear in games such as Fruit Slash Party. System Screen Mirroring, including AirPlay, sends the game display and any visible camera background to the receiver you select. System screen recording can also record what is visible. These user-selected system features are separate from the developer’s on-device processing. Stop Screen Mirroring in Control Center when you no longer want to share the display.

Purchases

Apple processes subscriptions and one-time purchases. MotionPlay reads verified StoreKit transaction information on your device only to determine whether premium games are unlocked. MotionPlay does not receive payment-card details or operate a developer purchase-history server. Apple handles Apple Account, billing and App Store data under Apple’s privacy policy.

Network and tracking

The games are included in the app and run locally. MotionPlay contains no third-party advertising SDK, analytics SDK or cross-app tracking. App Store purchases, restoring purchases, opening help links and system mirroring may use Apple or other services. Those services have their own privacy practices.

Your controls and data retention

MotionPlay does not create a player account or a persistent body-movement profile. You can revoke camera access in iOS or iPadOS Settings and choose touch controls instead. Purchase entitlements are supplied by Apple and may be restored using the purchasing Apple Account. Deleting the app does not cancel an Apple subscription; manage subscriptions through your Apple Account.

Shared household play

MotionPlay does not knowingly collect personal information from children through gameplay. An adult should choose a clear play area and supervise children. Camera permission and screen mirroring are controlled by the device user.

Support and privacy questions

If you choose to contact support by email, your address and message are used to respond to your request. Please do not send camera recordings, children’s images, passwords or payment-card information. Support correspondence is kept while needed to handle your request and any follow-up, then deleted unless a legal obligation requires retention. You may request deletion at the email address below. This website

These information pages do not use advertising, analytics scripts, cookies or contact forms. When you visit these pages on GitHub Pages, GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security purposes under GitHub’s privacy statement. This is separate from the on-device gameplay described above. Following external links or sending email is subject to those providers’ privacy practices.
