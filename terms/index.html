Play safely

Use a clear, stable, well-lit area. Secure your iPhone or iPad, leave space between players and stay away from televisions, furniture, stairs and other obstacles. An adult should supervise children. Stop if you feel pain, dizziness or discomfort. MotionPlay is entertainment and does not provide medical or fitness advice.

Free and premium games

Fruit Slash Party is available without a purchase. The other six listed games require verified premium access through a MotionPlay Pass subscription or a one-time lifetime unlock. The app displays the local App Store price and subscription period before you purchase.

MotionPlay Pass subscriptions

Monthly and yearly subscriptions provide the same premium game access for their respective billing periods. Payment is handled through your Apple Account. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled through Apple before renewal. See Apple’s purchase sheet for the applicable price, period and renewal information. Manage or cancel your subscription in Apple Account subscription settings. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Lifetime unlock and restoring access

The lifetime unlock is a one-time, non-consumable purchase for premium access. It does not renew. Use More → Restore purchases with the Apple Account that purchased access. Apple handles billing and refund requests. Family Sharing is not offered for these products in the current configuration.

Device and TV setup

Body controls need camera permission, a supported iPhone or iPad, suitable lighting and space to move. Touch controls provide an alternative. Recognition and responsiveness depend on conditions and device capabilities. Optional TV output uses system Screen Mirroring with a compatible AirPlay receiver; network and receiver conditions affect delay. MotionPlay cannot start system mirroring for you and does not include a tvOS app.
